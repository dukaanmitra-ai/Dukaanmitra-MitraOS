Mitra System usi WhatsApp ko storefront bana deta hai — customer ko catalogue, cart aur slot milta hai; aapko dashboard par revenue, top products, low stock aur 7-day forecast. ## 3. 2-minute pitch (English)

> "Most local businesses already sell on WhatsApp, but the catalogue, order history and follow-up live in the owner's head. Mitra System turns that chat into a proper storefront: searchable catalogue, cart, delivery or appointment slot, and a WhatsApp order message the shop already knows how to fulfil. On the owner side, the same system shows revenue, average order value, top products, low-stock alerts, customer segments and a 7-day demand forecast. It works for eight business types and nine cities out of the box, with local currency and delivery settings. We can start with one storefront and one dashboard, prove it for 14 days, then expand."

## 5. What is actually included

1. **Customer storefront** — catalogue, search, categories, cart, delivery/appointment slot, WhatsApp checkout.
2. **Owner dashboard** — revenue, orders, AOV, conversion, repeat rate, top products, recommendations, RFM segments, anomalies, low stock, source mix.
3. **Backend API** — catalog, insights, forecast, recommendations, lead scoring, orders, leads.
4. **ML-lite models** — WMA + day-of-week forecast, market-basket lift, weighted lead scoring, RFM, z-score anomalies. No external AI bill needed.
5. **Multi-city pricing** — 6 India cities + London, Dubai, New York, with local currency and delivery settings.
6. **Offline demo mode** — page works without backend; local model generates insights, orders save in the browser.

## 8. Objection handling

**"Mere paas already WhatsApp hai."**
> "Bilkul — hum WhatsApp hata nahi rahe. Usi chat ko catalogue, cart aur order history de rahe hain. Customer wahi number use karega, aapko bas structured order milega."

**"Shopify sasta hai."**
> "Shopify ka monthly plus theme plus apps plus payment fees jodte hain, aur WhatsApp flow alag se banana padta hai. Yahan setup ek baar, WhatsApp built-in, aur owner dashboard included. Pehle 14 din chala kar dekhein."

**"Mera staff nahi samjhega."**
> "Customer side sirf 3 step hai: item chuno, cart, WhatsApp. Owner side ek screen hai. Training 30 minute, video + WhatsApp support included."

**"Data safe hai?"**
> "Data aapka hai. Export on request. Frontend par koi API key nahi. Payment page par nahi — order WhatsApp par confirm hota hai. Production me Supabase/Postgres + role-based access."

**"Abhi budget nahi."**
> "Start with ₹14,999 website + WhatsApp lead system. Jab orders aane lagein, store aur dashboard upgrade kar lein. Lock-in nahi hai."

**"Kanpur ke bahar kaam karte ho?"**
> "Haan. 9 cities ke demo ready hain — Lucknow, Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, London, Dubai, New York. Delivery remote, on-site pilot optional."

## 9. Discovery questions (20 min)

1. Aaj order kaise aata hai — call, WhatsApp, walk-in, aggregator?
2. Din me kitne orders? Average order value?
3. Kaunse 5 items sabse zyada bikte hain?
4. Delivery karte ho ya pickup? Slot fix hai?
5. Payment kaise — cash, UPI, udhaar?
6. Repeat customers kitne %? Follow-up kaise hota hai?
7. Stock ka record kahan hai — register, Excel, ya yaad?
8. Kaun update karega menu/prices? Kitna time hai?
9. Abhi ka sabse bada operational pain kya hai?
10. 14 din ke pilot me kaunsa number improve hona chahiye?
