FAQ

Sawaal jo har dukaan wala puchta hai.

Mujhe coding ya website banani aati nahi. Chalega?

Haan. Setup hum karte hain. Aapko sirf apna product list, price aur timing dena hai. Baaki WhatsApp number aur storefront hum configure kar dete hain.

Customer ko naya app download karna padega?

Nahi. Customer wahi WhatsApp use karta hai jo abhi use kar raha hai. Aapko bas apna business number connect karwana hai.

AI galat price ya galat jawab de de to?

AI sirf aapki di hui catalogue se jawab deta hai. Jo cheez list me nahi hai, wo “owner ko bhej diya hai” bolke handover kar deta hai. Aap chahein to har reply approve karne ka mode bhi on kar sakte ho.

Hindi ke alawa aur languages?

Haan — English, Hinglish aur Urdu built-in hain. Naya city add karte waqt locale set karte ho, AI usi language me reply karta hai.

Payment kaise hota hai?

India me UPI/Razorpay, international me Stripe. Customer ko order ke saath payment link chala jata hai. Aap apne existing account se paisa receive karte ho.

Mera data kahan jata hai?

Aapka catalogue aur chats aapke Supabase project me store hote hain. AI ke liye Gemini/OpenRouter use hota hai, aur aap kabhi bhi model badal sakte ho.
