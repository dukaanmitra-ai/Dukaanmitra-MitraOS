FAQ

Seedhe jawab.

Kya ye sirf Kanpur ke liye hai?

Nahi. Kanpur pehla pilot hai kyunki wahan se humne shuru kiya. Delivery poore India me remote hai — Lucknow, Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad. Global clients ke liye London, Dubai aur New York bhi open hai.

Mujhe coding ya design nahi aati. Chalega?

Haan. Hum discovery, design, build, deploy aur training sab handle karte hain. Aapko sirf apna catalogue, prices, timing aur WhatsApp number dena hai.

Customer ko naya app download karna padega?

Nahi. Customer wahi WhatsApp use karta hai. StoreMitra me web store bhi hai, par order WhatsApp par bhi aa sakta hai. Aapko bas apna business number connect karwana hai.

AI apne aap payment ya stock change karta hai?

Nahi. Sensitive actions — payment, refund, price change, stock write-off — human approval gate ke peeche hain. AI recommend karta hai, audit trail banti hai, decision aapka rehta hai.

Data kahan store hota hai?

Aapke Supabase/Postgres project me, Row-Level Security ke saath. AI ke liye Gemini/OpenAI jaise models use hote hain, aur aap kabhi bhi model badal sakte ho. Chat transcripts optional hain.

Physical AI me exactly kya karte ho?

Sensor, camera, QR, printer ya machine se data lena, usko AI se samajhna, aur dashboard/alerts/actions me badalna. Example: cold-chain temperature alerts, solar telemetry, truck location, dukaan camera se footfall. Pilot 4 weeks me ek measurable use-case deliver karta hai.

Kitna time lagta hai?

Web launch: 2 weeks. StoreMitra: 3–4 weeks. AgentMitra: 2–4 weeks. Physical AI pilot: 4–6 weeks. Exact timeline discovery ke baad.
