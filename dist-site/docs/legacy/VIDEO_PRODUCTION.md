## 🎨 Video Brand Guidelines

### Color Palette

- **Primary**: Navy Blue (#0a0e27)
- **Secondary**: Gold (#c9a227)
- **Accent**: WhatsApp Green (#25D366)
- **Background**: Warm Paper (#f7f5f0)

### Typography

- **Headlines**: Space Grotesk Bold
- **Body**: Inter Regular
- **Captions**: Poppins Medium
- **Stats**: Fraunces Display

### Logo Usage

- Top-left corner (3-second minimum)
- Watermark in bottom-right (10% opacity)
- Fade in/out transitions
- Always on white or dark background

### Motion Graphics

- **Transitions**: Slide, fade, zoom (no jarring cuts)
- **Speed**: 0.5-1.0 seconds for transitions
- **Easing**: Ease-in-out
- **Direction**: Left-to-right for progress

## 📊 Video Performance Tracking

### Metrics to Monitor

**YouTube**:
- Views
- Watch time retention
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Subscriber growth
- Engagement (likes, comments)

**Website**:
- Video play rate
- Completion rate
- Conversion to signup
- Time on page

**Social Media**:
- Shares
- Saves
- Comments
- Hashtag performance

### Optimization

**A/B Testing**:
- Test different thumbnails
- Test intro hooks (0-15 seconds)
- Test CTAs (timing, wording)
- Test video lengths (2 min vs 3 min)

**Best Practices**:
- Custom thumbnail (no auto-generated)
- Clear title with keywords
- Compelling description
- Timestamps in description
- Pinned comment with CTA

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Engagement Boosters

1. **First 5 Seconds**: Hook immediately
   - "Running a shop shouldn't be complicated"
   - "What if AI ran your business?"

2. **Pattern Interrupts**: Change visuals every 5-7 seconds
   - Screen → Animation → Text → Screen

3. **Social Proof**: Show real results
   - "30% increase in repeat customers"
   - "500+ businesses already using DukaanMitra"

4. **Clear CTAs**: Every video ends with action
   - "Start free at dukaanmitra.in"
   - "Schedule your demo today"

### Quality Checklist

- [ ] Audio is clear (no background noise)
- [ ] Text is readable (large enough, high contrast)
- [ ] Cursor is visible (highlighted)
- [ ] Transitions are smooth (no jumps)
- [ ] Logo is present (branding)
- [ ] CTA is clear (next step)
- [ ] Subtitles are accurate (bilingual)
- [ ] Music is not overpowering (voice is primary)
